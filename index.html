Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 20, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect absolutely no data. This is a client-side only application. All data (webhook URLs, bot tokens, channel IDs, messages) is stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage.

2. How We Use Your Information

We don't collect or use any of your information. Data is only used locally in your browser to:

  • Send messages via Discord webhooks
  • Fetch messages via Discord bot API
  • Remember your settings between sessions

3. Data Storage

All data is stored locally in your browser's localStorage. This data is only accessible by you and never transmitted to any external server except Discord's official API endpoints when you explicitly send or fetch messages.

4. Third-Party Services

This Service integrates with Discord's API. When you send or fetch messages, data is transmitted directly to Discord's servers. Please refer to Discord's Privacy Policy for how they handle your data.

5. Cookies

This Service does not use cookies. All data is stored in localStorage, which is not sent to any server.

6. Your Rights

Since we don't collect any data, there is no data to access, modify, or delete on our servers. You can clear all locally stored data by:

  • Using the "clear chat log" button
  • Clearing your browser's localStorage
  • Deleting webhooks from the settings page

7. Security

Your bot tokens and webhook URLs are sensitive information. They are stored in localStorage, which is only accessible by your browser on your device. Keep your device secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us through Discord or GitHub.
